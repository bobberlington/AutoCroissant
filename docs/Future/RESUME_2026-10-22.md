## Lessons

- `/tmp` dies with a reboot: persistent paths only; bank WIP to branches often.
- A dead reviewer must fail the workflow, never count as "no findings".
- Many agents at once can hit a burst usage limit: run reviewers in small groups.
- Check every agent's result before pushing; keep a pre-push adversarial check for anything visual.
